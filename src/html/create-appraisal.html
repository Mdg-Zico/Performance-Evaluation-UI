Trust & Integrity

Supervision & Management

Gives clear directions and communicates performance expectations. Coaches, counsels and evaluates employees. Identifies and supports employee development opportunities.

Dependability

Is available for work on a consistent and timely basis with infrequent unplanned absences. Completes work in a timely manner. Meets commitment with minimal oversight.

Collaboration & Team Work

Consistently and proactively shares relevant information with others; Promotes team cooperation by valuing opinions and contributions of others; Manages conflict effectively

Organisational Success

Develops strategic action plans for practical use. Committed to continuous quality/process improvement. Commited to continous learning. Is creative and innovative.

Customer Orientation

Demonstrated customer focus by seeking out, understanding, and responding to the needs of both internal (co-workers) and external customers; Always takes ownership for delivering high quality services; effectively and professionally works with upset customers, solving their problems; Routinely seeks to secure quality, long-lasting customer relations.

Leadership Drive

Exhibits resourcefulness, independent action and professional judgement that are position appropriate; Develop and acts on appropriate problem solving methods. Develops other employees. Identifies and remedies situations that requires improvements at work.

Organisational Compliance & Safety

Adheres and leads the team in complying with all AEDC safety standards. Demonstrates appropriate use of and respect for organisation's property and equipment; Complies with the organisation's policies, regulations, rules, and procedures

Diversity & Respect

Rating Description

Below Average (50%)

Does not meet the required performance standards in key aspects of the job. Unacceptable Performance

Average (50-59%)

Level of productivity and general effectiveness meets the standards for this position. Quality and quantity of output are average. Improvement Needed

Good (60-69%)

Meets the required performance standards in all aspects of the job and sometimes exceeds the standards. Sustain Performance

Very Good (70-89%)

Meets all required performance standards and often exceeds the standards. Certainly ready for higher responsibility

Exceptional (90-100%)

Consistently and significantly exceeds all performance standards and job requirements. Delivers exceptional performance. Ready and should be given higher responsibility
